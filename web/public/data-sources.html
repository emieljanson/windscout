Inspection-only sources

The pinned Varun dataset is used only during local source research. Its records are not included in the released catalog while redistribution rights for the embedded location collection remain unconfirmed.

Forecast data

Forecasts are requested directly from Open-Meteo after you choose a location. Geoapify is used by maintainers for location validation and by the personal-spot flow; it is not queried while searching the curated catalog.
